What this system is for

Eye on the Coffee Farm helps you keep track of the health of the leaves in your coffee plots. You take a photo of one leaf, the system says if it looks healthy or sick, shows the damaged zone, and suggests what to do. Everything is saved, so you can see the history of each plot. The result is a help, not a diagnosis.

1. Create your account

  1. Open Create account, write a username (letters, numbers or _), your name, and a password of at least 8 characters with letters and numbers.
  2. New accounts are always coffee grower accounts. If you are a technician, ask the administrator to create your account.
  3. Log in with your username and password. Use Log out when you finish on a shared device.

2. Add your farm and your plots

  1. Go to Farms → Add a farm. Write the name, municipality and department.
  2. Open the farm and press Add a plot. Write the plot name, the variety type (resistant or susceptible to rust), the area in hectares and, if you know it, the planting year.
  3. The variety matters: the advice is different for resistant and susceptible varieties. If you do not know it, choose the closest option and ask a technician.

A plot with inspections cannot be deleted, and a farm with plots cannot be deleted, so that the history is not lost by mistake.

3. Check a leaf

  1. Open the plot and press Check a leaf.
  2. Take a photo of one single leaf, close up, on a plain background (your hand or a white sheet) and with good light.
  3. Send the photo. The analysis takes a few seconds. The result shows healthy or sick, the confidence, the zone with damage in magenta and an estimate of the rust level.
  4. If you see the warning This photo may not be suitable or the confidence is low, take a new photo.

4. Follow the actions

If the leaf looks sick, the system creates one or more actions (for example, check more leaves, or talk to a technician). Open the inspection and mark each action as in progress or done. An inspection has one of three states:

  • Waiting for review: a technician should look at it (the leaf looked sick, the confidence was low, or the photo was doubtful).
  • Action required: there are open actions.
  • Closed: nothing is pending.

A fungicide action can only be marked as done after a technician has reviewed the inspection. Only a technician can dismiss an action.

5. Tell us if the result was wrong

Under each result press Send feedback or report a problem. Say if the result was right, and write what happened. A technician answers and marks it as resolved. You can see your messages and the answers in Feedback.

What the system cannot do

  • It only knows coffee rust and red spider mite (RoCoLe dataset, robusta). Other problems can show up as false alarms.
  • The damaged zone and the rust level are estimates, not exact measures.
  • The advice comes from a simulation with assumptions and from Cenicafé's guidance. Always check more leaves and talk to a technician before using any product.
