How it works

  1. Register your farm and plots. Say where the farm is and which variety each plot has.
  2. Check a leaf. Take a photo of one leaf. The model says if it looks healthy or sick, marks the damaged zone and suggests what to do.
  3. A technician reviews. Sick or doubtful results are confirmed by a technician, and you follow the actions until the case is closed.

Who uses it

RoleWhat they do
Coffee growerRegisters farms and plots, checks leaves, follows actions, sends feedback.
TechnicianReviews inspections, answers feedback, reads reports and monitoring.
AdministratorManages users and watches the system and the model.
