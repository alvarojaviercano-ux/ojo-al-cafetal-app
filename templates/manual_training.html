Goal of the training

After this short training (about 45 minutes) each person can do their part of the process without help. It is done in a group or one to one, with the demo accounts and a few leaf photos.

Session 1 · Coffee growers (20 minutes)

  1. Explain what the system does and does not do (only rust and red spider mite; it is a help).
  2. Each grower creates an account, a farm and a plot, and chooses the variety type.
  3. Practice taking a good photo: one leaf, close, plain background, light. Then take a bad one (several leaves) and see the warning.
  4. Check a leaf, read the result, and mark an action as in progress.
  5. Send feedback about one result.

Check: the grower can find the history of their plot without help.

Session 2 · Technicians (15 minutes)

  1. Open Inspections and filter by Waiting for review.
  2. Open an inspection, look at the photo and save the review: healthy, sick or not sure. Explain what happens to the actions after the review.
  3. Answer and resolve a feedback message.
  4. Read the Monitoring page and explain what an alarm means.

Check: the technician reviews three inspections and resolves one message.

Session 3 · Administrator (10 minutes)

  1. Create a technician account and change a role.
  2. Read the event log, filter by errors, and find a failed login.
  3. Explain the weekly routine: open feedback, errors, agreement with technicians, drift and model version.

Material and support

  • The User manual and the Technical manual.
  • Questions and problems are sent from the Feedback page. Support is given by the administrator.
