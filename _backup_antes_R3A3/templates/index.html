How it works

Four quick steps, about 10 seconds in total.

  1. Take a photo.
    One single leaf, close up, on a plain background.
  2. The app cuts out the leaf.
    It separates the leaf from the background.
  3. The model decides.
    Healthy or sick, and how sure it is.
  4. You get advice.
    The damaged zone is marked in magenta, with a suggested action.
